Description

This easy, basic yeasted sweet bread recipe uses only eight ingredients and can be made into dinner rolls, cinnamon rolls, or sticky buns.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • ⅓ cup white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon active dry yeast
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ⅓ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided, or as needed

Steps

  1. Step 1: Combine milk and sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat. Heat until slightly warm to the touch then remove from heat and stir in yeast. Let stand until foamy, about 10 minutes.
  2. Step 2: Stir together flour and salt in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the dough hook. Add softened butter, eggs, and the yeast mixture and mix until dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
  3. Step 3: Turn dough out onto a floured surface and continue to knead until the dough is smooth and elastic, about 10 minutes.
  4. Step 4: Grease a large bowl with 1 tablespoon oil. Place the dough into the bowl and turn once to coat. Cover and let stand in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  5. Step 5: Grease a cookie sheet or 9x13-inch pan with remaining oil.
  6. Step 6: For plain rolls, punch down dough and divide into 12 equal pieces. Roll the pieces into balls and place them into the greased pan side by side.
  7. Step 7: Cover and let dough rise again until almost doubled in size, 20 to 25 minutes.
  8. Step 8: Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  9. Step 9: Bake in the preheated oven until rolls are golden brown, 20 to 25 minutes.
